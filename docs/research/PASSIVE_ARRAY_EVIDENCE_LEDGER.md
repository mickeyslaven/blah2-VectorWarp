# Passive-array research evidence ledger

Companion to [Professional single-site passive-radar arrays: evidence review and VectorWarp roadmap](PROFESSIONAL_PASSIVE_ARRAY_BENCHMARK.md). Accessed 2026-10-22 unless noted. Confidence refers to the narrow statement in the “supports” column, not every claim on the linked page.

Confidence scale:

- **High:** primary manufacturer/government/university source, peer-reviewed paper, patent text, or inspected repository code directly supports the statement.
- **Medium:** author/company technical article or repository abstract supports the statement, but full methods/data are incomplete or the material is partly promotional.
- **Low:** secondary source or inaccessible detail; retained only as context and not used for a design conclusion.

## Explicitly inaccessible or unverified facts

The research did not find public primary evidence sufficient to state any of the following:

- TwInvis, MAVERICK/OMNIGUARD, ELK-7080 or BATS element count, exact array geometry, channel topology, ADC resolution/effective dynamic range, installed calibration algorithm, pre-ADC cancellation network, production DOA algorithm, or covariance estimator;
- a technical identity or supplier relationship between the IAI and BATS systems despite similar public wording;
- classified/operational antenna internals from manufacturer photographs;
- a transferable numerical mutual-coupling coefficient for VectorWarp's elements/site;
- VectorWarp target-yield improvement from any proposed null, manifold, ECA, MUSIC, siting, filter, receiver or hardware change before the proposed held-out experiment.
